...Question: What are the stages of Alzheimer's dementia?
Answer: The stages of Alzheimer's disease dementia can be broken down into four categories: very mild, mild, moderate, and late/severe. In the very mild stage, an individual may struggle with just one complex activity of daily living, such as balancing a checkbook. However, their other activities of daily living remain normal. The mild stage is characterized by difficulty with several complex activities, like taking medications or making a sandwich. In the moderate stage, basic activities of daily living, including dressing, eating, toileting, and transferring, become challenging. Finally, in the late or severe stage, the person becomes dependent on others for all activities. They may need to be fed, experience incontinence, and spend most of their time in bed. These stages outline the progression of Alzheimer's disease dementia.
