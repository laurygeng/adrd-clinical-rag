...Question: What is caregiver burnout?
Answer: Caregiver burnout is a serious issue that can affect those who care for people with dementia. As caregivers take on more responsibilities, their stress levels can increase. While stress can sometimes provide motivation and a sense of purpose, the stress from caregiving is often ongoing and relentless. At a certain point, this constant pressure may lead to caregiver burnout. This can feel like not being yourself or not liking the person you have become. Signs of burnout can include being easily irritated with others, feeling tearful or frustrated, and not treating yourself kindly. Physical health can also suffer, with issues like high blood pressure, trouble sleeping, and other poor health outcomes. Caregivers may wake up feeling a sense of dread or wish they could escape their responsibilities, even though they know they can't. It is important to recognize these symptoms of burnout, as they indicate that the current caregiving situation is not sustainable. Seeking help and support is vital in addressing caregiver burnout.
