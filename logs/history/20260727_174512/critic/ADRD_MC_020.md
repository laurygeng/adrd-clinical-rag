Answer: For people with dementia, it is recommended to use a digital clock instead of a traditional round clock with numbers. The round clock requires abstract thinking to interpret the numbers, which can be difficult for someone with dementia. A digital clock with clear numbers is much easier to read and understand. Another option is a day clock that indicates if it is day or night by displaying a sun for daytime and a black color with stars and moons for nighttime. This can help individuals who struggle with determining the time of day. Additionally, there are clocks available that only display phrases like "morning," "afternoon," "evening," or "nighttime." These clocks are useful for individuals who may wake up in the middle of the night and feel disoriented, as they provide a clear indication of the time of day without requiring the ability to read a clock.
